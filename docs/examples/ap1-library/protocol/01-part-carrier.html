Part carrier

Protocol 1 of 7. The run starts here. Next is Primer plates.

Put each of 72 parts in its own pCR-Blunt II-TOPO, one part a well, so the collection keeps and BsmBI can release any of them again.

Materials

NameStoragePer runNoteSource
pCR-Blunt II-TOPO-20 °Cthe part carrier; its backbone carries no BsmBI site, which is what lets BsmBI release the part again later and leave the backbone whole
Zero Blunt TOPO PCR Cloning Kit-20 °Cone reaction per wellsupplies the carrier already linearised with topoisomerase I bound to each 3' end, so the reaction adds no ligase and no other enzyme[1]

Plates

Expected result

  • 72 carrier plasmids, one a part, each still named by the well it sits in.
  • No pool and no library: nothing here is mixed, so nothing has to be told apart afterwards.

Troubleshooting

Two parts end up in one well
The wells were pooled. One part a well is on purpose: a part that has met another cannot be re-cut back out on its own.

Notes

  • BsmBI releases a part from its carrier afterwards. The backbone carries no site of it, so a part comes back out on its own and the plasmid stays whole.
