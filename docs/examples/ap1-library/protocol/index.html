How this library is designed

One round appends one part list to every member of the library at once, so 3 rounds make 13824 constructs out of 72 synthesised parts.

The product keeps BbsI's sites, which is what lets the next round open it, and loses BsaI's with the external stuffers.

Your vector already carried an internal stuffer, so every part enters position N on AGGA, which is the overhang that stuffer spells: DNA that exists cannot be re-chosen.

The overhang standard changes 27 amino acid(s) across 26 part end(s); changes.tsv has wild type beside synthesised, and that is what you are about to pay for.

The last round needs 195,386 colonies for a 0.99 chance that none of its 13,824 products is missing, which is 14x its products. A round short of that floor loses members no later round can put back.

The finished barcode block is 41 bp and reads in the reverse of the order the rounds ran; barcodes.tsv says which barcode names which part.
