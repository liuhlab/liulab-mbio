Order and store

Expected result

  • One pool, 131 oligos, every one 350 nt and no length spread, which is what the padding is for.

Troubleshooting

The vendor bins the pool by length
Every oligo is padded to one length, so the spread is zero. A vendor asking for a different length wants this run's oligo length changed and the design run again.

Notes

  • pool.tsv names each oligo's block, which piece of it that is, and the primers that pull it out, so an oligo is traceable to its protein.
  • 131 oligos is the fewest the length budget allows for.

Caution: Spin the tube down before taking the cap off.

Expected result

  • One tube of pool in solution at 20 ng/µL or above, with nothing left undissolved on the wall of the tube.

Notes

  • Dividing by 20 is what the floor of at least 20 ng/µL comes to, and the amplification then pipettes 1 µL of 20 ng/µL.[1]
