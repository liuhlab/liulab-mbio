Pool the part lists

Expected result

  • N: one tube, 24 member(s), at least 1,000 ng at 22.3 ng/µL or above, 0.11 pmol of each member.
  • DBD: one tube, 24 member(s), at least 1,000 ng at 22.3 ng/µL or above, 0.219 pmol of each member.
  • C: one tube, 24 member(s), at least 1,000 ng at 22.3 ng/µL or above, 0.193 pmol of each member.

Troubleshooting

One member is much more dilute than the rest
Pool to the lowest member rather than to the mean; a member short here is short in every round after it.
The pool is below 22.3 ng/µL
Concentrate it, by SPRI at the round's own 2x ratio, or scale the digest up so the same mass arrives in a larger volume.

Notes

  • Library coverage is counted on equally represented members, so an uneven pool loses members that no later round can put back.
  • Equal picomoles are unequal masses: a short member weighs less than a long one for the same number of molecules, and weighing them equally would not pool them equally.
  • N: 0.11 pmol a member is 9.99 ng of its shortest at 147 bp and 78.1 ng of its longest at 1,149 bp.
  • DBD: 0.219 pmol a member is 39.2 ng of its shortest at 291 bp and 48.9 ng of its longest at 363 bp.
  • C: 0.193 pmol a member is 15.8 ng of its shortest at 133 bp and 125 ng of its longest at 1,048 bp.

Notes

  • Both steps run at 30 °C and not at 37 °C. That is a library precaution rather than an oversight.

  • The 0.99 is the project's own, and follows from the representation the screen downstream asks for. No source sets it, and nothing here defaults it; it works out at 8x this round's products.
  • The control measures the chain the design rests on — two cuts, a blunt chopper, a ligase that refuses blunt ends and the 2x clean-up — rather than assuming it.

Read the library back

  • H28 no sourced number — the linkage read: no pass mark for the linkage read: what share of reads must carry a barcode that still names its part the method has not decided Filled by the project, or a source that sets a mark rather than reporting what it reached.

Expected result

  • A table from barcode combination to cargo: each read decodes to one member of each part list, and the coding bases it carries are that member's.
  • The 41 bp block reads C, DBD and N, each barcode separated from the last by the cloning scar TTCC, at 1401-1442 of the representative construct.

Troubleshooting

A read carries fewer barcodes than there are rounds
A round did not go in. Check that round's prep against its own record before blaming the sequencing.
A combination decodes to a member the coding bases are not
That round joined a block to the wrong barcode, so barcodes.tsv no longer names what the library holds. Rebuild that round rather than carrying the table forward; no later round repairs it.

Notes

  • The block reads in the reverse of the order the rounds ran: each round inserted its barcode ahead of the ones already there.
  • 0.0% of the single-base deletions a barcode can carry leave a read another barcode of the same part list could leave, which no read can be assigned through.
  • Takacsi-Nagy's Figures 1D and 1E read about 95% of their reads carrying a valid barcode at every position, and nearly 90% of the library correctly linked. That is what one source reached, not a mark this library is held to.

Expected result

  • A count for each of up to 13,824 distinct combinations: how many of them are seen at all, and how evenly they are read.
  • A combination with no reads is a member the library has lost.
  • At least 99.5% of the combinations seen; a 90th/10th percentile skew ratio below 10, judged at 100 or more reads a member, which is 1,382,400 reads over 13,824 combinations.

Troubleshooting

A combination is missing
It was lost at a round or at a bottleneck since, and no later step puts it back. Read the rounds' own titre plates before re-reading this one.
The counts are heavily skewed
Members differ in length and a bottleneck can favour the short ones. Imkeller's Table 2 prices the skew in screen coverage: a 90th/10th ratio of 2.5 wants 200-fold, 5 wants 300-fold and 10 wants 400-fold, so a skewed library costs cells downstream rather than failing here.

Notes

  • Read representation again after every later bottleneck — the final assembly, and anything downstream that resamples the library. Linkage is read once; this one is read at each.
  • The short amplicon is what makes repeating it cheap: linkage spans the whole cargo, this spans the block.
  • Where you amplify the block to read it, carry any sample index on a primer rather than ligating it on, and keep the barcodes away from where a primer anneals: both cost more read counts than what a barcode spells does.
