Move the library across

Expected result

  • The whole 1076 bp cargo free, on AGGA and TTCC, out of 6435 bp of library.
  • PmeI cuts the backbone it came out of, so that backbone cannot close again.

Troubleshooting

The library backbone survives the assembly
The blunt enzyme missed, or the heat kill was short. Both show up as colonies carrying the round's own vector rather than the working one.

Notes

  • One tube, two stages. Killing the releasing enzymes before the working vector goes in is what keeps them off its backbone.

  • H24 no sourced number — final assembly, the one-pot reaction: no mass for the one-pot assembly: the cargo arrives unpurified, so neither the working vector's mass nor the ratio it meets the cargo at is sourced the method has not decided Filled by a pilot.

Expected result

  • One circular final vector a member, the ccdB cassette displaced by the cargo.

Notes

  • This is the one reaction where the working vector meets material the rounds made; the rounds all finish first.

Caution: Keep the cells and the cuvette on ice; a warm cuvette arcs.

Expected result

  • At least 195,386 net colonies: the floor for the 0.99 chance the project asked for that none of its 13,824 distinct members is missing, equally represented.
  • At that count the chance a named member is missing is 7.27 × 10⁻⁷.
  • Near-empty plates from a no-cargo control beside it; what grows there is working vector that kept its ccdB cassette.

Troubleshooting

Fewer net colonies than the count above
The library has lost members in the transfer. Nothing downstream puts them back; repeat the assembly from more of the released cargo.

Notes

  • This is a bottleneck like a round's, and the library can only lose members here. Electroporate all of the assembly rather than a measured part of it.
  • The settings are the cells' own and are keyed by their catalogue number, not set by this method.

Read the library back

Expected result

  • The same combinations, at a similar evenness. A combination seen before the move and not after it was lost in the transfer into the working vector.
  • At least 99.5% of what survived the move seen; a 90th/10th percentile skew ratio below 10, judged at 100 or more reads a member, which is 1,382,400 reads over 13,824 combinations.

Troubleshooting

Markedly fewer combinations than before the move
The transfer was the bottleneck, not the rounds. Compare the colony count with the step above before rebuilding anything.

Notes

  • Linkage is read once, in the library backbone; a barcode still names the same part after the move, because the move carries the whole cargo in one piece.
  • The forward anchor is the same retained internal stuffer, which travels with the cargo; the reverse anchor moved with the vector, so this pair is designed against the final record rather than reused from the read before the move.

This protocol

  1. Pick the working vector
  2. Release the cargo with BsaI and PmeI
  3. Assemble the cargo into the working vector
  4. Clean the assembly up and electroporate into Endura ElectroCompetent Cells
  5. Read representation in the final vector
