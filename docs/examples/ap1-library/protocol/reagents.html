Selective plates for the final transferFinal cargo ligation
  • T7 DNA Ligase: never PEG Never add PEG to a T7 ligase reaction. Blunt-end ligation is forced above 20% (w/v) PEG 6000, and StickTogether buffer already carries 7.5%; the refusal that removes escapees holds only while nothing adds more.[13]
  • T7 DNA Ligase: never heat inactivation Do not heat-inactivate T7 DNA ligase in PEG: transformation will be inhibited. Go from the ligation straight to the clean-up.[13]

Caution: Keep the polymerase on ice.

Caution: Keep the cells and the cuvette on ice; a warm cuvette arcs.
